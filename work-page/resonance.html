RESONANCE (2026)

Resonance (w/ Alyssa Durdey, Elisa Gonçalves de Andrade, Jaden Thompson) is an interactive sound installation that consists of a wooden frame record player and display place accompanied by a collection of seven clear acrylic vinyls, each depicting layers of brain tissue engraved onto them to reconstruct the 3D volume. Each vinyl can be played on the record player and each produces audio influenced by the 2D image engraved inside them via LEDs and photoresistors. By detecting the light variations passing through the engraved vinyls, the sound becomes transfigured. By turning biological data into a tactile and auditory experience, Resonance invites the audience to engage with the staggering complexity of their own internal architecture.

Resonance was exhibited at the (McGill) Neuro for the 2026 end-of-year Art & Neuroscience Convergence exhibition.
