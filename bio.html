BIO WORKS

Rebecca Acone is an artist and designer based in Tiohtià:ke/Montreal whose main focus is on interactive tangible UI/UX and the intersection of humans and machines. Coupled with her recent studies in Computation Arts and Philosophy at Concordia University, her careful practice of observation allows her to pay close attention to how people interact with systems to inform designs that are intuitive and responsive. This practice plays a key role in her process and experimentation as her work often explores the implications arising from these observations, questioning how interfaces shape perception, behavior and our understanding of reality. Her toolkit mainly includes fabricated interactive surfaces, electronic circuits, and algorithmic systems for live visual performance. Driven by an interest in Human-Machine Interaction, she experiments with how design can facilitate meaningful and emotionally resonant connections between users and technology while reflecting on human presence in a nuanced way.

She’s exhibited and performed in institutions and venues such as at World Creation Studio, Societe des arts technologiques (SAT), Concordia University’s Black Box and 4th Space, La Sotterenea, and NOMADlife.tv. She started exhibiting her work in 2023, and has since continued installation work and has further explored live performing visuals. She’s received recognition for her collaborative and interactive installation works at the D/CART end of year exhibition Nodes.Noeuds and at MUSICMOTION’s HACKLAB24, both held at the SAT. She has also been an active member of collectives, including the Computation Arts Student Society and Sandbox Collective, where she has collaborated with members on creative projects and curation, and contributed to community-driven events. She currently holds a BFA in the Computation Arts Specialization with a Minor in Philosophy (Concordia University, Tiohtià:ke/Montreal, QC CAN).
